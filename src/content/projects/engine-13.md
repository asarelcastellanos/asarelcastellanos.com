## The pitch

A physics-ragdoll co-op game for PC. Firefighters try to save the town while a superhero and a villain wreck it around them. Engine 13 is a codename.

## What works today

The game is in its early stages. It has a main menu, a pause menu, a first-person player, and keyboard and gamepad input.

## What's next

Online co-op for 4 to 8 players through Steam lobbies, ragdoll characters, fire and hose, and a destructible town.
